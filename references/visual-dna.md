# Visual DNA

## Goal
Compress the scene into the smallest useful set of creative decisions.

Visual DNA is the persistent identity layer for the whole edition.

## Template
```yaml
subject:
core_relationship:
signature:
composition:
memory:
mood:
line:
color:
texture:
```

## Rules
- Keep it compact.
- Describe relationships, not inventories.
- Separate subject identity from rendering style.
- Favor the memorable over the comprehensive.
- Avoid long lists of optional objects.

## Example
```yaml
subject: small group of lakeside bald cypress trees
core_relationship: one tall central tree with smaller surrounding trees emerging from shallow water
signature: trees standing directly in the lake
composition: small vertical tree cluster against broad horizontal space
memory: a quiet lakeside moment that feels slow and slightly nostalgic
mood: quiet, poetic, playful, unhurried
line: fine, loose, lightly irregular ink
color: muted olive, pale blue, warm earth
texture: warm paper with sparse translucent washes
```
