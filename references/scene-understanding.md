# Scene Understanding

## Goal
Understand the photograph as a complete visual scene before deciding how to stylize it.

## Read in this order
1. Whole composition: what occupies the frame and where is the visual weight?
2. Spatial layers: foreground, midground, background, horizon, negative space.
3. Primary subject: what does the eye read first?
4. Subject relationships: alignment, grouping, interaction, scale, repetition, rhythm.
5. Light: direction, softness, contrast, time-of-day feeling.
6. Color atmosphere: dominant temperature, muted/bright quality, color relationships.
7. Signature: what makes this specific photograph recognizable?
8. Memory: what single visual moment or emotional impression should survive reduction?

## Important distinction
Scene Map describes what is there.
Visual DNA describes what the artwork should remember.
Do not merge them.

## Example for the lakeside photo
```yaml
space: broad lake and sky with a narrow shoreline
foreground: shallow water, reflections, shoreline texture
midground: group of trees emerging from shallow water
background: distant mountain and open sky
subject: bald cypress trees
anchor: tall central tree
relationships: central tall tree surrounded by smaller trees; trees connect visually through waterline and reflections
depth: water → trees/shore → distant mountain → sky
light: soft, diffuse daylight
color_atmosphere: muted blue, green and warm earth
signature: trees growing directly out of the lake
mood: quiet, spacious, slow, slightly nostalgic
```
