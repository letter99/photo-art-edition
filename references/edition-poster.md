# A — Editorial Art Poster

## Deliverable
One independent poster asset whose ratio follows the source orientation.

## Layout
Choose a layout family from the source photo's orientation and usable visual
weight. Preserve the source's dominant spatial gesture; do not force a fixed
vertical poster on every input.

- Portrait source: vertical 3:4 poster, usually with photograph and Master in
  two stacked zones of approximately equal height.
- Landscape source: horizontal 4:3 or 3:2 poster, usually with photograph and
  Master in two side-by-side zones, or a broad photo band above a shorter
  illustration band when the horizon is important.
- Panoramic source: horizontal 3:2 canvas with a wider photographic zone;
  never crop away the visual anchor merely to satisfy the default ratio.

Record the chosen canvas ratio and zone arrangement before generation.

## Photograph treatment
Preserve real structure, proportion, material, light and color atmosphere. Only light editorial grading is appropriate.

## Illustration treatment
Place the locked master illustration. Do not regenerate or reinterpret it.

## Typography
Optional. Use very little text, only when it strengthens the art-book/editorial character. Remove it from sticker derivatives unless it belongs inherently to the illustration.

## Short generation/assembly instruction
> Create an editorial poster using the supplied original photograph and locked master illustration. Choose the canvas ratio and zone arrangement from the source orientation: portrait 3:4 stacked, landscape 4:3 or 3:2 side-by-side or carefully stacked, panoramic 3:2 wide. Keep the photo realistic and lightly graded, preserve the visual anchor, and maintain a quiet literary art-book feel.
