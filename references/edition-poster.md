# A — Editorial Art Poster

## Deliverable
One independent vertical 3:4 poster asset.

## Layout
Default: two approximately equal vertical zones by height.
- upper zone: original photograph
- lower zone: locked Master Illustration

## Photograph treatment
Preserve real structure, proportion, material, light and color atmosphere. Only light editorial grading is appropriate.

## Illustration treatment
Place the locked master illustration. Do not regenerate or reinterpret it.

## Typography
Optional. Use very little text, only when it strengthens the art-book/editorial character. Remove it from sticker derivatives unless it belongs inherently to the illustration.

## Short generation/assembly instruction
> Create the 3:4 editorial poster using the supplied original photograph and locked master illustration. Keep the photo realistic and lightly graded. Place the master illustration as the lower half with generous paper space. Maintain a quiet, literary art-book feel.
